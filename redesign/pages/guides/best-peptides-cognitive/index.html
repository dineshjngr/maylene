How to Choose the Right Peptide

Use this framework to evaluate options consistently before ordering.

When selecting peptides for your research, consider the following factors:

  • Research Objectives: Match the peptide to your specific research goals and mechanisms of interest.
  • Purity Standards: Always verify purity through certificates of analysis (COA).
  • Storage Requirements: Ensure you can maintain proper storage conditions.
  • Supplier Reputation: Purchase from trusted sources with transparent testing.
  • Documentation: Look for comprehensive product documentation and references.

Sources & evidence

These papers describe the stated compounds and study models. They do not test or certify Maylene products. Animal or cell findings do not establish human benefit, and pharmaceutical trials do not validate research-only pens.

  • Semax and BDNF signalling — Dolotov et al., 2006

    Rat hippocampal and behavioural experiments; not proof of cognitive improvement in people.

  • Selank and phenazepam comparison — Medvedev et al., 2014

    Comparative clinical study of 60 patients. The abstract does not establish long-term safety or equivalence to all anxiety treatments.

  • BPC-157 and Achilles tendon healing — Staresinic et al., 2003

    Rat tendon-injury and cell experiments; this is not a human treatment trial.

  • BPC-157 and growth-hormone receptors — Chang et al., 2014

    Rat tendon fibroblasts; receptor-expression findings do not establish clinical benefit.
