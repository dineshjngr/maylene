Three simple steps

Know what the numbers mean.

This is a conversion reference. It does not select an amount or recommend a treatment.

  1. 01

    Match your pen

    Select the product and its labelled content. Different strengths can have different conversions.

  2. 02

    Enter one value

    Type an amount in mg or a number of clicks. For blends, only the published reference points are available.

  3. 03

    Check the reference

    Read the conversion alongside the instructions for your exact pen. Fractional clicks are arithmetic results, not selectable dial settings.

What happens when I select a bundle?

Each pen keeps its own strength and conversion. Choose a component pen to calculate it individually; the total mg across a bundle is not a pen strength.

Why are some values unavailable?

Glow, Wolverine and the Retatrutide + Cagrilintide blend use a limited set of published reference points. Values between these points are not estimated. We do not have a confirmed conversion for Somatropin.

Where do the conversions come from?

The values were checked against the published reference for these pens on 5 October 2026. They are a reference, not independent device calibration. Always check the instructions supplied with your exact pen.
