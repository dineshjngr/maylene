Semax vs Selank: Nootropic Peptide Comparison

Semax and Selank are both Russian-developed heptapeptides with regulatory approval in Russia, but they target different aspects of brain function. Semax primarily enhances cognition through BDNF upregulation, while Selank reduces anxiety through GABA modulation. They are commonly studied individually and in combination, as their mechanisms are complementary rather than overlapping.

This comparison is structured to highlight practical differences in research context, mechanisms, and use-case fit.

By Maylene · Sources and page updated 7 October 2026

Semax

A synthetic ACTH(4-10) analog that potently upregulates BDNF and enhances cognitive function. Approved in Russia for cognitive disorders and stroke recovery. Does not affect the HPA axis despite its ACTH origin.

Selank

A synthetic tuftsin analog with anxiolytic effects comparable to benzodiazepines but without sedation or dependency. Approved in Russia for anxiety disorders. Also has immunomodulatory properties from its tuftsin backbone.

Different Targets, Complementary Effects

Semax and Selank were developed by the same research institute but designed to address different neurological needs. Semax targets the neurotrophic system, dramatically increasing BDNF levels which support neuronal survival, synaptic plasticity, and learning. Selank targets the GABAergic system, stabilizing inhibitory signaling and reducing anxiety without the cognitive impairment that benzodiazepines cause. Because cognitive performance and anxiety are related but distinct, combining them is a logical research direction.

The BDNF Connection

BDNF is arguably the most important neurotrophin for learning and memory. Semax is one of the most potent known stimulators of BDNF expression, increasing it 2 to 8 fold in hippocampal tissue after a single dose. Selank also increases BDNF, but to a lesser degree. For research specifically focused on neuroplasticity and cognitive enhancement, Semax has the stronger evidence base. For research where anxiety is the primary variable, Selank is more directly relevant.

Safety and Clinical Track Record

Both peptides have been used clinically in Russia for over a decade, providing a real-world safety database that most research peptides lack. Neither produces tolerance, dependency, or significant side effects in published clinical data. Both are administered intranasally, which provides rapid CNS access. The lack of hormonal effects is notable. Semax does not activate the HPA axis despite being derived from ACTH, and Selank has no endocrine effects.

The Verdict

For cognitive enhancement research, Semax is the stronger choice due to its potent BDNF upregulation. For anxiety-focused research, Selank provides direct anxiolytic effects without cognitive compromise. The combination is popular among researchers because the mechanisms are complementary, addressing both cognitive performance and the anxiety that often impairs it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Semax and Selank be studied together?

Yes, and many researchers do exactly this. Their mechanisms are complementary: Semax enhances cognition through BDNF while Selank reduces anxiety through GABA. There are no reported adverse interactions.

Which is better for memory research?

Semax, due to its stronger BDNF upregulation. BDNF is directly involved in long-term potentiation, the cellular mechanism underlying memory formation.

Do either cause dependency?

No. Neither Semax nor Selank has shown tolerance development or dependency in published studies, including clinical use data from Russia. This distinguishes Selank from benzodiazepines.

How are they administered?

Both are typically administered intranasally in published research. This route provides good bioavailability and rapid access to the central nervous system.
