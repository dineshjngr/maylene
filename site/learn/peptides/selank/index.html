Selank: Selank (Anxiolytic Heptapeptide)

Category: Cognitive & Neurological

Selank is a synthetic analog of the naturally occurring immunomodulatory peptide tuftsin, with an additional sequence that provides enhanced stability and neurological activity. Like Semax, it was developed at the Institute of Molecular Genetics in Russia and has been approved as a prescription medication in Russia for anxiety and related disorders.

This guide compiles key literature themes, handling context, and practical comparison points for research-focused evaluation.

By Maylene · Sources and page updated 7 October 2026

Overview

The peptide's mechanism centers on GABA system modulation, producing anxiolytic effects that researchers have compared to benzodiazepines but without the sedation, cognitive impairment, or dependence potential. This clean anxiolytic profile has generated significant research interest internationally.

Discovery & Background

Selank was developed through the 1990s and 2000s by researchers at the Russian Academy of Sciences, building on earlier work with the immunopeptide tuftsin. The goal was to create a compound with anxiolytic properties that lacked the drawbacks of existing medications. After extensive preclinical work, it received regulatory approval in Russia and has been available as a nasal spray since the early 2010s.

Mechanism of Action

Selank modulates the GABAergic system and influences serotonin metabolism, producing anxiolytic effects without sedation or dependency. It also has immunomodulatory properties inherited from its tuftsin parent molecule.

  • 2

  • 3

  • 4

  • 5

  • 6

Research Benefits

The benefit areas below reflect commonly discussed research interests for this peptide.

Anxiolytic Effects

Reduces anxiety-like behavior in animal models with potency comparable to benzodiazepines, but without sedation or dependency.

Cognitive Support

Studies show improvements in learning and memory alongside anxiety reduction, a combination not typical of traditional anxiolytics.

Immunomodulation

Modulates immune function through its tuftsin-derived structure, potentially useful in psychoneuroimmunology research.

No Dependency

Published research shows no tolerance development, physical dependence, or withdrawal effects with sustained use.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Selank?

Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide based on the natural immunopeptide tuftsin with an added Pro-Gly-Pro sequence. It was developed in Russia as an anxiolytic without the drawbacks of benzodiazepines.

How does Selank reduce anxiety?

Selank modulates the GABA system and stabilizes enkephalins, the body's natural anti-anxiety peptides. Unlike benzodiazepines, it does not cause sedation, cognitive impairment, or physical dependence.

Can Selank and Semax be studied together?

Yes. Some researchers study them in combination because they have complementary mechanisms. Semax primarily enhances cognition through BDNF, while Selank targets anxiety through GABA modulation.

What is tuftsin?

Tuftsin is a naturally occurring tetrapeptide (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg) involved in immune function. It stimulates phagocytosis and immune cell activity. Selank was built on the tuftsin backbone with additional amino acids.

How is Selank administered in research?

Like Semax, Selank is typically administered intranasally in published studies. This route provides rapid absorption and good bioavailability for CNS research.
