How to Use the Pen (Quick Guide)

  • Wash hands thoroughly before use.
  • Remove cap, check solution is clear, and attach a new needle.
  • Prime the pen to remove air.
  • Select the dose using the dial and confirm in the display window.
  • Inject at a 90° angle into the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm.
  • Hold for 5–10 seconds, then remove and safely dispose of the needle.

Rotate injection sites weekly.
