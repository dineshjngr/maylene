Wholesale FAQ

Common questions from first-time wholesale buyers and institutional teams.

How quickly can we receive a wholesale quote?

Most enquiries receive a custom quote within 24 business hours. Complex requests may require additional specification details.

Can you support recurring procurement schedules?

Yes. Professional and Enterprise accounts can be set up for repeat ordering patterns with account-level support.

Do wholesale orders still include batch documentation?

Confirm which records are available for each product and batch before accepting a quote. Include your documentation requirements in the enquiry; wholesale pricing does not establish that a particular report is available.
